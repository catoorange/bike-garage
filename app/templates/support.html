🛠️ Using Bike Garage

Bike Garage stores your frame geometry, draws your bikes to scale, and lets you compare them side by side. Everything below works in any browser, on any device. (although it is under development so ymmv)

Getting started

Create an account, then open Profile and enter your measurements. These drive the rider figure in the fit overlay — if you skip this, the rider will look wrong. You only need to do it once.

Adding a bike

My bikes → Add bike. Choosing a preset fills in the geometry for you; you can correct any number afterwards. The fields that actually change the drawing are head tube angle and length, reach, stack, chainstay length, and wheel size. The rest are nice to have. Crank length and angle live in your profile and apply to every bike.

Comparing bikes

Compare overlays two bikes at once. One draws solid, the other as a ghost, so you can see exactly where they differ — usually the front end. The rider figure follows your profile, so you can judge how a different frame would actually sit.

Gearing

Gearing calculates speed at a given cadence for your chainrings and cassette. Fill in one or two front rings and your cogs, biggest first. Blank fields use sensible defaults. The km/h / mph choice is remembered on your profile.

Photos

Add photos to any bike — the first one becomes the thumbnail on your bike list. You can also set a profile picture. jpg, png, webp or gif, up to 5 MB each.

💬 Questions & bug reports

Something confusing, or not behaving the way you expect? Send it here and I'll take a look. Tell me which page you were on, what you did, and what you expected to happen instead — a screenshot helps more than you'd think. Your email is optional, but I can't write back without it.
